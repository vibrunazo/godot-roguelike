# LORE.md - Game Lore, Theme & Aesthetic Guide

This document is a general style and theme reference for the game. Future agents and contributors should use it to ensure new assets, items, and content fit the overall game theme and atmosphere.

---

## 1. Core Premise & Theme

- **Genre:** 3D top-down action roguelite.
- **Origins:** The project literally started from a GameDev.tv Godot 3D tutorial course.
- **The Main Theme:** Meta jokes about game dev tutorials, hobby gamedev, and game jam culture.
- **The Narrative & Roguelite Loop:** You play as a placeholder hero in a gamedev tutorial who is forever stuck in **Tutorial Hell**. Every run is another loop through the tutorial project, hence the roguelite structure.
- **Potential Game Titles Being Considered:**
  - *Placeholder Hero*
  - *Tutorial Hell*
  - *Reload Current Scene*

---

## 2. World & Asset Rationale

The setting reflects its actual reality: an unfinished tutorial project.

- **Arbitrary Prototype Assets:** Enemies, props, and environment pieces are placed arbitrarily across levels because they are prototype assets chosen with minimal effort for a tutorial course. That literal reality is the joke.
- **Prototyping Environment:** Levels use prototype grid tiles, simple modular walls, and haphazardly placed sample assets (such as couches, tables, barrels, and checkpoint flags).
- **The Loop:** Defeat and restarts reflect reloading the tutorial scene.

---

## 3. The Protagonist

- **The Placeholder Hero:** A generic low-poly mannequin character with a retro CRT monitor head and an antenna, standing in as the temporary player character that never got replaced with a custom model.

---

## 4. In-Game Items & Upgrades

In-game items follow the tutorial and hobby dev theme:

- **Tutorial & Dev Books for Spells:** New abilities are learned by reading developer books found in the world.
  - Introductory spells: *"...for Dummies"* style books (e.g. *Fireball for Dummies*).
  - Advanced upgrades: *"...The Good Parts"* style books with an O'Reilly-style animal cover (e.g. *Fireball, The Good Parts*).
- **Hobby Gamedev & Game Jam Culture:** Items, upgrades, and flavor text draw from memes and tropes of hobby game development, tutorial bingeing, and game jams.
- **AI & Vibe Coding Jokes:** Items and upgrades can poke fun at AI usage in game development—jokes about "vibe coding", hallucinated methods, blind prompt-engineering, and bizarre AI-generated assets or glue code.

---

## 5. Visual Art Style

- **KayKit Low-Poly:** Follows the KayKit asset style with low-polygon geometry, clean shapes, and friendly, cozy graphics.
- **Aesthetic:** Bright, welcoming, and colorful prototyping look rather than gritty or realistic.
